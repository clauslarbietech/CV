FitLife AI Coach — Support

Home workout coaching for iPhone and web. v1.0.0

Contact

Email support@fitlife.app for help, bug reports, or privacy requests. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Getting started

  1. Open FitLife and complete onboarding (accept the health disclaimer).
  2. On My Stuff, tap Start workout.
  3. Use Settings (gear icon) for units, legal info, or to delete your data.

What v1.0 includes

  • Guided home workouts (Iron 14, Iron 30, Long Train)
  • Nutrition notes and beta Food Scan estimates
  • Coach chat (scripted beta replies — not live AI)
  • Local progress tracking on your device

Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Delete all data on this device. For cloud account deletion (if you signed in with sync enabled), email support@fitlife.app.
