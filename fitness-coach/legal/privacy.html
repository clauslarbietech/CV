Privacy Policy

FitLife AI Coach · Effective August 2026 · v1.0.0

Overview

FitLife helps you track home workouts, nutrition notes, and wellness habits. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

Data we store

Profile info you enter (name, goals, weight, sex), workout progress, notes, med checklists, and chat messages are stored on your device. If you sign in with Supabase when configured, account data may sync to our database.

Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party ad tracking in v1.0.

Photos (Food Scan beta)

Food photos you pick are processed on-device for beta macro estimates. We do not upload food images to a server in this version unless you later enable cloud features.

Children

FitLife is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

In the app, open Settings → Delete all data on this device to wipe workouts, notes, chat, and profile stored locally. For cloud account deletion (if sync is enabled), email support@fitlife.app.

Contact

Email support@fitlife.app for privacy requests.
