Accomplishments

People with dyslexia who shaped the world.

  • Entrepreneurship

    Richard Branson

    Founded the Virgin Group across music, travel, and more. He credits dyslexia with teaching him to simplify messages and delegate well.

    “Dyslexia helped me on the creative side with simplifying things.”

  • Fashion & investing

    Daymond John

    Built FUBU into a major urban apparel brand and became a Shark Tank investor. He turned creative–analytical thinking into brand mastery.

    “Dream big and think bigger.”

  • Film

    Steven Spielberg

    One of cinema’s most influential directors. Visual storytelling and imagination powered a career that reshaped modern film.

  • Acting & comedy

    Whoopi Goldberg

    EGOT winner whose career spans stage, screen, and television. She has spoken openly about dyslexia and learning differently.

  • Acting

    Octavia Spencer

    Academy Award–winning actor who has discussed dyslexia and the creative strengths that come with thinking in pictures.

  • Finance

    Charles Schwab

    Built one of America’s best-known brokerage firms. He has advocated for dyslexia awareness in education and business.

  • Retail design

    Ingvar Kamprad

    Founded IKEA and scaled a global design-for-everyone vision rooted in clarity, systems, and practical problem-solving.

  • Ocean exploration

    Robert Ballard

    Led the team that found the Titanic and dozens of wrecks. He later said dyslexia shaped how he maps and sees underwater worlds.

    “My dyslexia helped me find wrecks in the darkness of the ocean.”

  • Technology

    Alex Karp

    CEO of Palantir. He has described dyslexia as forcing free thinking because there is no standard playbook to memorize.

    “There is no playbook a dyslexic can master. Therefore we learn to think freely.”

  • Acting

    Keira Knightley

    Acclaimed actor who has spoken about dyslexia and building a career through determination, listening, and craft.

  • Sports & business

    Magic Johnson

    NBA legend and later a major entrepreneur. His story highlights leadership, spatial skill, and long-term vision.

  • Science

    Carol Greider

    Nobel Prize–winning molecular biologist whose work on telomeres advanced our understanding of chromosomes and aging.
